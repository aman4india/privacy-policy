Aman Singh ("we", "us", or "our") operates the Diney-POS mobile application (the "Service"). This page informs you of our policies regarding the collection, use, and disclosure of personal data when you use our Service and the choices you have associated with that data.

1. Information Collection and Use

Diney-POS is a Business-to-Business (B2B) restaurant management system designed to operate primarily on a local network (communicating with your self-hosted server/database). We do not collect, store, or process personal data on external third-party servers.

Data created within the app, such as:

  • Waiter Names or IDs
  • Restaurant Menu Items and Prices
  • Table Configurations
  • Customer Order Details

This information is transmitted directly to and stored on your own local database (e.g., your local MySQL/PHP server). We do not have access to this data.

2. Log Data & Analytics

We want to inform you that whenever you use our Service, in a case of an error in the app, we may collect data and information (through third-party products like Google Play Console) on your phone called Log Data. This Log Data may include information such as your device Internet Protocol ("IP") address, device name, operating system version, the configuration of the app when utilizing our Service, the time and date of your use of the Service, and other statistics. This data is used solely to fix bugs and improve app stability.

3. Device Permissions

Depending on your use of the app, Diney-POS may request the following permissions:

  • Network Access: Required to communicate with your restaurant's local server/database to sync menus and orders.

4. Security

We value your trust in using our app. Since Diney-POS transmits data to your own local server, the security of that data relies on the security of your internal network configuration. We strongly advise securing your local Wi-Fi and server with strong passwords and firewalls.

5. Children's Privacy

These Services do not address anyone under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children under 13.
