POSOMS Privacy Policy (Google Play Listing)[cite: 1]

Last updated: 2026-03-09[cite: 1]

This Privacy Policy explains how POSOMS (“we”, “our”, “us”) collects, uses, and protects information when you use the POSOMS mobile application available on Google Play[cite: 1]. It summarizes the data practices relevant to our mobile experience in clear, non-technical language[cite: 1].

1. Data We Collect[cite: 1]

Category[cite: 1] Details[cite: 1] Purpose[cite: 1]
Account Information[cite: 1] Staff names, email addresses, role assignments, and outlet associations[cite: 1]. Creating and maintaining staff logins, enforcing permissions, and providing secure access[cite: 1].
Restaurant Operations Data[cite: 1] Orders, bills, menu items, tables, shifts, printer settings, and similar outlet-level configuration[cite: 1]. Powering day-to-day POS features, analytics, and reports[cite: 1].
Diagnostics (Optional)[cite: 1] Basic performance and crash information if you opt into sharing diagnostics[cite: 1]. Monitoring app reliability and resolving technical issues[cite: 1].
Notification Tokens[cite: 1] Device tokens for push notifications when the feature is enabled[cite: 1]. Sending order alerts and operational notifications[cite: 1].

We do not collect consumer payment card numbers or access your device contacts, photos, or sensors[cite: 1].

2. How We Use Data[cite: 1]

  1. Account Access – To authenticate staff members and apply the correct permissions[cite: 1].
  2. Business Operations – To process orders, manage tables, track staff activity, and produce reports[cite: 1].
  3. Realtime Updates – To keep order and table information synchronized across staff devices[cite: 1].
  4. Notifications – To deliver push notifications about important restaurant events when enabled[cite: 1].
  5. Compliance & Auditing – To maintain records required for restaurant accountability and regulatory obligations[cite: 1].

3. Data Sharing & Disclosure[cite: 1]

We do not sell your personal data[cite: 1]. We share information only with trusted service providers that help us deliver the app, such as:[cite: 1]

  • Cloud Hosting & Storage Providers – To host our application services and databases[cite: 1].
  • Authentication & Notification Services – To manage secure sign-in and send push notifications[cite: 1].
  • Analytics & Diagnostics Providers – To help us monitor performance and resolve issues[cite: 1].

These partners are bound by confidentiality and data protection obligations[cite: 1]. We may also disclose information if required by law, to respond to legal requests, or to protect our rights and users[cite: 1].

4. Data Storage & Security[cite: 1]

  • Encryption – Data is encrypted during transfer between your device and our servers[cite: 1].
  • Access Controls – Only authorized personnel and systems can access operational data[cite: 1].
  • Segregation – Restaurant data is logically separated so outlets cannot access each other’s information[cite: 1].
  • Session Controls – Access tokens are validated and refreshed to prevent unauthorized use[cite: 1].

5. Data Retention[cite: 1]

  • Restaurant operational data is retained while the restaurant maintains an active subscription or as required by applicable regulations[cite: 1].
  • Staff account information remains until a restaurant administrator deletes the account[cite: 1].
  • Diagnostic logs are kept only as long as needed for troubleshooting and are then securely deleted or anonymized[cite: 1].

6. Your Choices & Rights[cite: 1]

Depending on your location and employer’s policies, you may be able to:[cite: 1]

  • Access or update your account information[cite: 1].
  • Request deletion of your staff account (subject to restaurant admin approval)[cite: 1].
  • Export operational data through the in-app reports or administrator tools[cite: 1].

Restaurant administrators control staff roles, outlet assignments, and permissions from the POSOMS portal[cite: 1]. Staff members should contact their administrator to exercise these rights[cite: 1].
